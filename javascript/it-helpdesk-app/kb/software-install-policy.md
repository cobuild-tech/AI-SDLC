# Software install policy

## Self-service software portal

Most approved software can be installed without a ticket from the software portal on your laptop. This includes Zoom, Slack, Figma, VS Code, Adobe Acrobat Reader, 7-Zip and Google Chrome.

1. Open the Software Portal app.
2. Search for the application.
3. Select Install. It usually finishes within 10 minutes.

## Software not in the portal

1. Raise a ticket with the name of the software, a link to the vendor, and the business reason.
2. The service desk checks it against the security review list. New software typically takes 5 working days to review.
3. Paid software also needs cost-centre approval from your manager.

## Licensed design and engineering tools

Figma paid seats and Adobe Creative Cloud licences are assigned by team. Installing the app from the portal is free; a paid seat must be requested by your team lead.

## What you must not install

Do not install browser extensions that read page content, remote-access tools (such as TeamViewer or AnyDesk), or unapproved AI assistants that upload company data. The security team removes these automatically.
