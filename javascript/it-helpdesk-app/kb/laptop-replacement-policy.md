# Laptop replacement policy

## Replacement cycle

Laptops are replaced every 4 years. If your laptop is 4 years old or older, you are eligible for a replacement; raise a ticket and the hardware team will schedule a swap.

## Hardware faults

1. Restart the laptop and install pending updates.
2. For a flickering screen, check whether it changes when you move the lid. Flicker that follows lid movement is usually a loose display cable and needs a repair.
3. For short battery life, check the battery health report. Batteries below 70% health are replaced under the hardware budget.
4. Raise a ticket with photos or a short video of the fault. A loaner laptop is available while yours is repaired.

## Lost or stolen laptops

Report a lost or stolen laptop immediately as an urgent ticket. The security team will remotely lock and wipe the device. Also report it to your manager.

## New starter laptops

Managers request new starter laptops at least 5 working days before the start date. The hardware team images the laptop with the standard department apps and delivers it on the first morning.
